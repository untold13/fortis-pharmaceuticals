# Fortis drug dossier review

The two supplied archives contain the same 44 dossiers in PDF and DOCX formats. The PDF text was reviewed one file at a time. Product information was added only when the medicine, strength and dosage form could be matched to a published catalog product. Clinical statements were kept within the boundaries of the product page's cited FDA, EMA or journal references. The original PDFs were not published or attached.

## Result

Twenty-four published product records received a bilingual product-information section covering formulation, use and safety, and storage. Five published products were left unchanged because the supplied dossier did not match their exact strength or release designation. Twenty-three dossiers had no corresponding published product, and one general norgestrel dossier was superseded by the exact-strength document.
